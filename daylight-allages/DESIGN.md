# Design System: Daylight — all-ages POC

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "One Record, Three Printings"**

One family record, set three times by three different print shops. The domain, the
flows and every shared component are written once; `data-world` on `.phone`
swaps a complete token fill, and the same list of obligations comes out as a
newspaper weather page, a mid-century picture book, or a departures board. The
three worlds are peers. None is a theme of another, and none is a colour variant:
they differ in type, ground, density, line weight, corner language and signature
move.

The product is read by six-year-olds and by adults in the same minute, so
legibility is solved structurally rather than stylistically. No world softens
into cartoon. Every state is printed as a word **and** a colour **and** a shape at
the same time, so the screen survives greyscale, a colour-blind parent and a
pre-reader. "Nobody has this" is a material — 45° hatching and a dashed edge —
not an absence. Icons are one drawn 24-box SVG set at 1.75 stroke; there are no
emoji and no font glyphs anywhere in the product.

Density is phone-native and unapologetically informational: 390×844, a flat
scrolling column under a world masthead, a 76px tab bar, and rows that carry
time, state, owner and place without a card around them (except in Primer, where
the card *is* the row). Decoration is near zero; what looks like ornament — the
daylight band, the key, the status plate — is always reporting data.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Three complete worlds behind one token contract, switched at runtime
- State = word + colour + shape, always, simultaneously
- Hatching and a dashed edge mean "open / nobody"
- One drawn SVG icon set, 1.75 stroke, 24-box; no emoji, no glyph fonts
- Flat informational density; type does the hierarchy, not elevation
- Light grounds for the two reading worlds, near-black for the glance world

## Colors

Three palettes, one role map. Every world fills the identical set of custom
properties — ground, surface, rule, three inks, accent, nav chrome, six state
inks with six paired washes, four person colours — so a shared component never
knows which world it is in.

### Primary
- **Almanac Ochre** (`almanac-ochre`): the almanac's one accent. Chips when
  selected, the caret, the active week day, and `almanac-ochre-ink` for accented
  text on newsprint where the raw ochre would be too light on light.
- **Primer Flat Blue** (`primer-blue`): the picture book's single flat ink
  accent. It fills the entire header block, not a detail of it.
- **Departures Signal Amber** (`departures-amber`): the signage accent. Reserved
  for the thing that still needs a human — `waiting` plates, the active tab, the
  focus ring, selection.

### Secondary
- **Almanac Slate** (`almanac-slate`): the masthead and tab-bar ground. The only
  dark field in a light world, and it carries `almanac-masthead-amber` for the
  active mark and lede emphasis.
- **Primer Black Line** (`primer-line`): not a neutral — a drawn ink. It is the
  same value as the body ink and it is the border colour of every container in
  the world at 2.5px.

### Neutral
- **Newsprint / Sheet** (`almanac-newsprint`, `almanac-sheet`,
  `almanac-sheet-sunk`): warm ground, warmer sheet, sunk tone for keys, strips
  and the message card.
- **Primer Paper / Sheet** (`primer-paper`, `primer-sheet`, `primer-sheet-sunk`):
  off-white ground with pure-white cards; the card is whiter than the page.
- **Departures Night / Panel** (`departures-night`, `departures-panel`,
  `departures-panel-2`, `departures-nav-black`): the only world where the ground
  is darker than its surfaces. Row hover brightens *up* into `departures-panel`.
- **Ink ramp** (`*-ink`, `*-ink-2`, `*-ink-3`): title, body, and fine/meta
  respectively, in all three worlds. Three steps, never a fourth.
- **Rules** (`*-rule`, `*-rule-soft`): the hard rule bounds a region; the soft
  rule separates list rows. Primer's hard rule is the black line.

### Named Rules
**The Three-Carrier Rule.** Every state — `unowned`, `waiting`, `accepted`,
`declined`, `asked-out`, `handled` — is carried by a word (`STATUS_WORD` in
`src/domain/rules.ts`: No one / Waiting / Taken / Said no / Asked out / Handled),
a colour (`--s-*`), and a shape (`.mark--*`, and dashed vs. solid edges) at the
same time. No state may ever be carried by colour alone, in any world, on any
surface. The audit test: desaturate the screen and read it; if a state became
unreadable, the surface is wrong.

**The Hatch Rule.** 45° hatching (`--hatch`) plus a dashed edge means "open — the
record has no owner". It is the texture of `unowned` in every world: the mark, the
empty person mark, the Almanac pin, the Primer card band. Hatching is never
decorative, and never used for anything else.

**The Wash Pairing Rule.** A state ink is never used as a fill behind text. Each
`--s-*` ink ships with an `--s-*-wash` ground, and that pair is the only legal
combination — except in Departures, where `waiting` deliberately inverts to solid
amber with `departures-on-amber` text because the glance screen wants one siren.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Barlow Condensed (Almanac) / Lexend (Primer) / Public Sans
(Departures)
**Body Font:** Barlow (Almanac) / Lexend (Primer) / Public Sans (Departures)
**Label/Mono Font:** Roboto Mono (Almanac); Primer and Departures set labels in
their own single family — no third voice.

**Character:** Almanac is a condensed newspaper masthead over a humanist text
face with a monospaced data column; Primer is one open, round-but-not-cute family
doing every job, which is what keeps the picture book adult; Departures is a
single grotesk pushed to 800 weight for distance reading.

### Hierarchy
- **H1** (700, 34px, 1.02, -0.028em, balanced): the screen name in the masthead.
  Almanac raises it to 38px, Departures drops to 32px/800, Primer stays 34px at
  -0.03em.
- **H2** (700, 20px, 1.12): sheet titles. Departures' lead-panel title is the
  world's own H2 at 26px/800/-0.032em.
- **Title** (600, 17px, 1.25, -0.016em): the name of an obligation in a row.
  Primer's card title runs larger at 19px/600/-0.028em.
- **Body** (400, 14.5px, 1.4, `ink-2`): ledes, sentences, comments, message
  bodies. Ledes are capped at 28–32ch.
- **Fine** (400, 13px, 1.35, `ink-3`): meta, hints, timestamps in prose.
- **Label** (700, 11px, 0.13em, uppercase, `ink-3`): section heads, field labels,
  the kind tag, fact-list terms. **Primer overrides every label** to sentence
  case at 11.5–13px with little or no tracking.
- **Data** (700, 13.5px, tabular): clock times and counts, in `--font-data`.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Column Rule.** Any number that sits in a column — clock times, day
numbers, elapsed counts, the message timestamp — carries `.tnum`
(`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). Times must not shift width between rows.

**The No-Shouting-In-Primer Rule.** Uppercase tracked micro-type is Almanac's and
Departures' voice. In Primer, `[data-world='primer']` resets every label,
divider, tab and state word to sentence case — because a picture book sets its
captions, it does not stamp them.

**The Lede Rule.** Every masthead carries one line of what is actually true right
now, in body size, capped at 28–32ch, with the single most important noun in
`<b>` and the world's accent colour. One bold phrase per lede, never two.

## Layout

A fixed 390×844 phone column: masthead (world-owned) → signature move → optional
permanent key → scrolling list → 76px tab bar with a 12px bottom inset. The
device frame and the world/role switcher in `src/styles/frame.css` are a demo rig
that sits outside the product and is not part of this system.

Horizontal rhythm is a single gutter: **18px** for shared screen content, rows,
sheet bodies and section heads; **16px** where a world sets its own edge (Primer
cards, Departures header and board). Vertical rhythm is `26px` above a section
label, `10px` under it, `13–14px` row padding, `8–12px` between siblings, `46px`
of air inside an empty state.

Lists are full-bleed rows divided by `--rule-soft`, with no outer container —
except Primer, where `[data-world='primer'] .rows` drops the shared top rule and
each row becomes a free-standing card with a 16px side margin and 14px gap.
Almanac and Departures rows are CSS grids with a fixed time column
(`50px` / `54px`) so times align down the page; Departures reserves a fixed
`84px` end column so owner and status plate never reflow.

**The One-Column Rule.** There is no multi-column layout and no responsive
breakpoint in this product. Everything is one 390px column; density is solved by
row height, not by splitting the page.

## Elevation & Depth

Near-flat by intent. Depth is carried by ground tone, rules and hatching, not by
lift. Only three things in the product cast a shadow: cards (`--shadow-1`), a
Primer card on hover (`--shadow-2`), and the bottom sheet (`--shadow-sheet`).
Rows, headers, keys, strips, plates and buttons are completely flat at every
state; their state change is a background tone swap.

The backdrop behind a sheet is a single flat `rgba(10, 16, 18, .42)` in all three
worlds — the only shared colour literal in the system.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Resting card** (`--shadow-1`): a card or the Departures lead panel at rest.
  Tuned per world — a soft double-layer on newsprint, a near-opaque pair on
  near-black.
- **Lifted card** (`--shadow-2`): Primer card hover only.
- **Sheet** (`--shadow-sheet`): the upward shadow under a bottom sheet, strong
  enough to detach the sheet from the plan behind it.

### Named Rules
**The Flat-Row Rule.** A list row never lifts. `.row:hover` and `.dep-row:hover`
change background only, and an unanswered row is marked by a wash
(`.row--open` → `--s-waiting-wash`), never by elevation.

**The Tone-Over-Lift Rule.** When a surface needs to read as a different plane,
change its ground (`--surface-2`, `--surface-sunk`, a state wash) and give it a
rule. Handled is a destination, so the whole screen takes
`--s-handled-wash` as its ground; it does not take a shadow.

## Shapes

Corner language is a world-level decision and one of the three strongest
signals between them. Almanac is almost-square printed stock (2/3/4px, 6px on the
sheet); Primer is **absolutely square** (0px everywhere, with a 99px pill
reserved for chips alone); Departures is signage-crisp (1/2px, 3px on the sheet).
Line weight follows: Almanac and Departures draw 1px hairlines and 1.5px state
edges, while Primer sets `--line: 2.5px` and every container, band, tab underline
and checkbox in that world inherits it.

Circles are reserved and meaningful: the 22px state mark, the 30px person mark,
the 27px Almanac band pin, the Primer person-token, the 9px history dot and the
9px Departures owner dot. A circle in this product is always a person or a state,
never a button and never decoration.

**The Dashed-Edge Rule.** A dashed border means unowned. It appears on
`.state--unowned`, `.mark--unowned`, `.person--none`, `.alm-pin--unowned` and
`.pri-token.is-empty`, and nowhere else.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** the world's medium corner (`--r-md`: 3px / 0px / 2px), 1.5px border
  (2.5px in Primer), 46px tall, 16px side padding, 8px icon gap.
- **Yes:** solid `--s-accepted` with white text; hover darkens the same ink 14%
  via `color-mix`. The accept action wears the accepted state's colour.
- **No:** transparent with a `--s-declined` edge and ink; hover fills with
  `--s-declined-wash`. Declining is never styled as damage.
- **Accent:** solid `--accent` on `--on-accent`, for the one forward action on a
  sheet.
- **Plain:** transparent with a `--rule` edge; hover fills `--surface-2`.
- **Quiet:** 40px, no edge, `ink-2`, 14px — inline and secondary only.
- **Icon button:** a 44px square box with a centred 20–22px drawn icon,
  `--r-md`, hover fills `--surface-2`. The box is 44px even when the glyph is 18.
- **Disabled:** `opacity: .45` and `cursor: not-allowed`; colour is not changed.

### Chips
- **Style:** `--surface` ground, `--rule` edge, `--r-pill` (2px in Almanac and
  Departures, a true 99px pill in Primer), 42px tall, 14.5px/500.
- **State:** selected (`aria-pressed="true"` or `.is-on`) fills `--accent` with
  `--on-accent` and goes to 600. A trailing `.chip__n` count sits in
  `--font-data` at 11px/700, 72% opacity.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** `--r-lg` (4px / 0px / 2px).
- **Background:** `--surface`, with `--rule` at `--line` weight.
- **Shadow Strategy:** `--shadow-1` at rest (see Elevation).
- **Internal Padding:** 13–15px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** full-width, 46px minimum, 11px/13px padding, `--surface` ground,
  `--rule` edge at `--line`, `--r-md`. Textarea 92px, vertical resize only.
- **Focus:** the border shifts to `--accent` *and* the world focus ring draws
  outside it. Placeholder is `ink-3`.
- **Label:** the Label role above, 7px clear; hint is `fine` 7px below.

### Navigation
- **Style:** a 76px bottom tab bar on `--nav-bg` with a `--nav-rule` top edge and
  a 12px bottom inset. Four tabs, each a drawn icon over a 10px/700/0.1em
  uppercase caption in `--font-data` (sentence case at 11px in Primer).
- **States:** `--nav-dim` at rest → `--nav-ink-2` on hover →
  `aria-current="page"` goes `--nav-on` and draws a 30px × 2.5px bar at the top
  of the tab. Primer widens that bar to the full tab at `--line`.
- **Badge:** a count pill on `--s-declined` with white text and a 1.5px
  `--nav-bg` border, so it reads as cut out of the bar.

### The status carriers (signature, shared)
- **State word** (`.state`): the state's word in its own ink on its own wash with
  a 1.5px edge of the same ink; 11px/700/0.09em uppercase in `--font-data`.
  Unowned is dashed. Primer sets it 12px sentence case at `--line`; Departures
  tracks it to 0.14em at 1px radius and inverts `waiting` to solid amber.
- **Mark** (`.mark`): the same six states as a 22px circle (17px `.mark--sm`) with
  a 2px edge, carrying a drawn check for accepted/handled and a cross for
  declined, both at stroke 3. It ships `role="img"` with the status word as its
  label, so the shape is never the only carrier for assistive tech either.
- **Person mark** (`.person`): a 30px circle on one of four `--people-*` inks with
  the initial at 42% of its size. `.person--none` is hatched with a dashed
  unowned edge and a drawn person icon; `.person--helper` takes the `asked-out`
  ink, because an outside helper is a state, not a family member.

### Almanac: the daylight band and the key
The day drawn as a forecast strip. A 62px track of `--band-night` holds a
`--band-day` block from sun-up to sun-down, 1px hour ticks at 9/12/15/18 with
8.5px mono hour marks, and every timed obligation pinned at its hour as a 27px
circle in its state colour — the hour as a number, or the drawn car for a ride,
unowned ones hatched and dashed. Untimed items are reported in a footnote rather
than faked onto the track.

Beneath it the **key is permanent**: four cells (Taken / Waiting / No one / Said
no), each a small mark over an 8.5px uppercase mono caption, divided by soft
rules on `--surface-2`. The key is the all-ages affordance — learn it once, and
it stays on screen. It is never collapsed, hidden behind a tooltip, or reduced to
three symbols.

### Primer: the person-token and the head band
The token is a flat two-ink glyph: a circle with a `--rule` edge at 6.2% of its
size, a body block filling the bottom 46% in the person's ink, and a head circle
at 40% of the size in `--ink`. The same glyph is used at 48px in the family row
and 30px in a card band. The empty token is dashed, sits on `--surface-2`, and
prints a `?` at 40% size in the unowned ink — "nobody" stands in the line beside
the family rather than being omitted.

A Primer card carries a **flat ink head band** across its full width — token,
state word, time — tinted with the state's wash (hatched when unowned) and
separated from the body by a 2.5px line. This world commits colour at region
scale.

### Departures: the status plate
The next unanswered obligation is raised out of the board into a lead panel with
`--shadow-1`: the state word as a plate, then how long it has sat
("asked Taylor 2h ago"), then the title at 26px/800, then time · place · for
whom. Elapsed time is the pressure; there is no nag copy and no red alarm. When
nothing is waiting, the panel keeps its shape and prints the accepted mark with
"Everything today has an answer." Board rows reserve a fixed 84px end column for
owner-dot + name over mark + 9px uppercase state word, so the right edge of the
board is a straight line.

### Sheets
Every secondary task is a bottom sheet, never a blocking modal: `--surface`, top
corners at `--r-sheet`, `max-height: 84%`, `--shadow-sheet`, a 16/18px head with a
44px close button, a scrolling body, and a foot that respects
`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`. It rises 28px over `--slow` while the backdrop
fades over `--base`; Escape closes it and focus lands on the dialog itself.

### Motion
Three durations only — `--fast: 150ms` (colour and border transitions),
`--base: 240ms` (backdrop fade), `--slow: 380ms` (sheet rise) — all on
`--ease: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`
all three collapse to `1ms` and every animation, transition and scroll behaviour
is forced flat. Nothing in the product moves except a sheet arriving and a colour
changing.

### Focus
`:focus` is cleared and `:focus-visible` always draws the world's own ring:
Almanac 2px slate at 2px offset, Primer 3px black ink at 2px offset, Departures
2px signal amber at 2px offset. A focus ring is never removed, and a world's ring
is never substituted for another's.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** print every state with its word, its colour and its shape at once —
  `STATUS_WORD` for the word, `--s-*` for the colour, `.mark--*` or a dashed edge
  for the shape.
- **Do** take the state word from `STATUS_WORD` (No one / Waiting / Taken / Said
  no / Asked out / Handled). A world may lengthen it into a sentence
  ("Waiting on Taylor"), never rename the state.
- **Do** style a new shared component entirely from the token contract
  (`--ink`, `--surface`, `--rule`, `--line`, `--r-*`, `--s-*`, `--font-*`), so it
  works in all three worlds unseen.
- **Do** put world-specific overrides behind `[data-world='…']` in the shared
  stylesheet or in the world's own CSS file.
- **Do** give every interactive control a box of at least 44px (`.iconBtn` 44px,
  `.btn` 46px, `.input` 46px, `.check__row` 46px, `.pickRow` 60px).
- **Do** mark hatching + a dashed edge as the only way to say "nobody has this".
- **Do** draw new icons into `src/ui/Icon.tsx` on the 24 box at 1.75 stroke with
  round caps and joins.
- **Do** add `.tnum` to any number that sits in a column.
- **Do** keep the three motion durations and let them collapse to 1ms under
  reduced motion.
- **Do** cap a lede at 28–32ch and bold exactly one phrase in it.
- **Do** use `color-mix(in srgb, …)` against an existing token for a hover
  darkening, rather than introducing a new hex.

### Don't:
- **Don't** let colour alone carry a state, a count, or an owner — not in a dot,
  not in a stripe, not in a bar.
- **Don't** ship an emoji or an icon-font glyph. Every glyph in the product comes
  from the drawn set.
- **Don't** give a Primer card a coloured side stripe or a rounded corner; that
  world states colour as a flat head band at region scale and keeps every corner
  at 0px.
- **Don't** soften a radius to signal friendliness. The radius belongs to the
  world (`--r-*`); legibility is the all-ages mechanism, roundness is not.
- **Don't** lift a list row, a header, a key or a plate on hover. Change its
  ground.
- **Don't** mix a state ink with a surface other than its paired `--s-*-wash`.
- **Don't** add a fourth ink step, a fifth person colour, or a second accent to a
  world.
- **Don't** collapse, hide or shorten the Almanac key — it is permanent and
  four-symbol.
- **Don't** replace a bottom sheet with a blocking centred modal.
- **Don't** remove a focus ring or borrow another world's ring colour.
- **Don't** introduce a hex literal in a component stylesheet; the only shared
  literals in the product are `#fff` on a state fill and the sheet backdrop.
- **Don't** treat the device frame or the world/role rig (`frame.css`) as part of
  the product's visual system.
